# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Finance analysts working with Copilot to investigate historical business metrics,
produce forecasts and maintain them across refreshes.

## Product Purpose

Turn selected time-series data into explainable, reproducible forecasts. The
agent investigates before asking for configuration, tests bounded improvements
and presents evidence for a human adoption decision.

## Positioning

A Copilot-native analysis workspace over the reusable Python/Nixtla modeling,
chronological evaluation, research and forecasting-ledger capabilities.

## Operating Context

The primary interface is a local Copilot canvas. CLI/Python use and portable
forecast artifacts remain supported. The existing FINN Agent Workbench is the
user-selected interaction and visual-quality reference, not a runtime dependency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve statistical, ML, optional smooth, hierarchy, normalization, driver,
custom-model and external comparison capabilities. Make ensembles authored,
evaluated and explicitly adopted candidates. Local analysis is bounded and
cancelable. New access, expensive/external execution, adoption and publishing
retain explicit authorization boundaries.

Forecasts, scenarios, planning targets, observed actuals and estimated values
remain distinct. Missing observations are not silently converted to zeros.
Prediction intervals require their own evidence.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the project's independent identity. Match FINN's restrained, legible
workstation quality; replace the incumbent terminal-like Streamlit presentation.
Do not imply affiliation or predictive superiority.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing example datasets, golden scenario fixtures, model/backtest artifacts,
research receipts and `docs/workbench` screenshots. Synthetic demonstrations
must be labeled as such.

## Product Principles

- Investigate before asking; make inferred assumptions visible and reversible.
- One forecasting engine and one shared user/agent state.
- Compare like-for-like chronological evidence, not attractive charts.
- Adopt deliberately into immutable, reproducible versions.
- Keep operational detail available without making it the starting experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-operable analysis and exact-value alternatives to
charts. Support host light/dark preference, narrow canvases and reduced motion.
